Frequently Asked Questions

📍Where do I park, and how do I get in?
On weekends, the entire parking lot right outside the venue is free, and below is a photo so you can visualize it when you pull into the lot — otherwise, a safe bet is alongside Strathcona Park on Prior Street. Tap to enlarge ⤢
Werklab · 1275 Venables St, East Vancouver →
Is there a bar?
Yes — we’ll serve a limited quantity of wellness-focused alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks. If you’re picky about what you drink, bring your own and pop it in our fridge (just be open to sharing, or to someone mixing your bottle up with theirs).
Is there food?
Yep — we’ll have healthy snacks to keep you fuelled through the night, but we definitely recommend grabbing an early dinner. Want to join some of the attendees beforehand at Superflux Beer Company from 5:00–6:45 PM? Text Paul at 604-809-3188 to RSVP.
Do I need to know anyone?
To buy a ticket, you need to be referred — we’ve carefully curated the event to match the right people up and make sure we have the right people in the room.
What do I wear, and is there a coat check?
Wear something you can lie down in for the meditation and, of course, dance in. If you feel like bringing a change of clothes, you can do that and even leave it at the coat check.
Do I need to bring a yoga mat for the sound bath meditation?
Nope — we have 40 yoga mats available, plus plenty of seats if you’d rather not lie down.
Can I just come for the music?
One ticket covers the whole night. Come when you like, but the earlier part is where you meet people — and later, you party with a little more purpose and connection than the usual show.
Where are charity net proceeds going?
Based on popular vote (when you buy your ticket), we send 100% of net proceeds to the charity the community votes on. We also encourage donating privately to any of these great charities that promote physical, mental, and financial wellness for people in need.
