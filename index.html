8:30 PMGround Yourself

“Sonic Acupunkture” Sound Bath Meditation Breathe

You may listen to Huberman and read Atomic Habits. You like the idea of flow states but want a new way to get into one — meet Sonic Acupunkture.

Sonic Acupunkture is a guided meditation built on a sound bath, scored by someone who engineers the music himself. Charles “OceanFox” Morgan tunes the frequencies and polyrhythms deliberately, borrowing the audio techniques that make video games hard to put down and pointing them at focus instead. The practice underneath is bhavana, a Buddhist meditation tradition that works through breath and sound.

Lie back, sit up, or stay in a chair. The breathing is gentle — nothing strenuous, nothing physical.

Feeling like meeting people instead? Pop into the co-working space next door for healthy snacks and connect deeper with people in and outside your breakout group.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a bar?
Not as of now, but you can BYOB and save the money — there’s a fridge :) We’ll have a welcome drink on us.
Do I need to know anyone?
To buy a ticket, yes — we’ve carefully curated the event to match the right people up and make sure we have the right people in the room. If you want to work something out and go solo, call Paul to explore a fit: 604-809-3188.
What do I wear, and is there coat check?
Wear something you can lie down in for the meditation and, of course, dance in. If you feel like bringing a change of clothes, you can do that and even leave it at coat check.
Can I just come for the music?
One ticket covers the whole night. Come when you like, but the earlier part is where you meet people — and later, you party with a little more purpose and connection than the usual show.
Where are charity net proceeds going?
Based on popular vote (when you buy your ticket), we send 100% of net proceeds to the charity the community votes on. We also encourage donating privately to any of these great charities that promote physical, mental, and financial wellness for people in need.
