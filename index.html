The Through-Line

A festival that re-energizes bold doers by connecting them through wellness and an EDM night that makes you lift both hands in the air and feel unstoppable.

To level up in life, it’s all about unleashing your most alive self and making bold bets — with the right people rooting for you — not through playing it safe, being a little more efficient, or static networking.

As entrepreneurs, coaches, wellness experts, and any other hardworking professionals, it’s easy to feel inspired one day, then lose energy as the days get darker and busy work piles up.

Until suddenly you play a great EDM song in a full car and everyone feels instantly uplifted and more hopeful. Or you remember that exercise high that takes you by surprise and you feel unstoppable. It’s these tiny bursts of energy that turn around your day.

EDM music and wellness in good company create bursts of energy that, over time, fuel us to create things that matter — even if our rational minds (or AI) say “not now.”

AI will never understand it, but it’s these moments of human energy that fuel boldness, human bonding, and random good deeds — overriding our optimizing minds with the powerful empathy deep in our silent hearts.

We need these bursts of human energy to fight our adversaries, create things that endure, and counter the atrophy of life — to never let our relationships wither, no matter how grown-up life makes us be.

Boldness, bonding, and giving our hearts a voice will bring back hope in our world. Saving the world can feel overwhelming — but it doesn’t need to be. We just need to break it into little bits, and have an amazing time doing it.

This is a small invite to level up as a human simply by being as human as you can. Raise a glass and raise your hands as we dance the night away with purpose.

Amongst humans and music that bring us back to life — one beat at a time.
